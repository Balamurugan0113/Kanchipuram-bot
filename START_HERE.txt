📝 LOGGING (Members & Managers)
   /log_grind      - Log grinding activity
   /log_craft      - Log crafting activity
   /log_sale       - Log sales activity

📦 STORAGE (Members & Managers)
   /storage_add    - Add items to storage
   /storage_remove - Remove items from storage
   /storage_view   - View current storage status

📊 STATISTICS (Members & Managers)
   /stats          - Overall business statistics
   /user_stats     - Individual user contribution

🛠️ ADMIN COMMANDS (Managers Only)
   /admin_clear_logs     - Clear all transaction logs
   /admin_reset_storage  - Reset all inventory
   /admin_clear_user     - Remove specific user's data
